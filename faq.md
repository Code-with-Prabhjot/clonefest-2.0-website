# CloneFest 2.0 - Event Details & FAQ

**What is CloneFest?**
CloneFest is an intensive 10-day open-source sprint designed to elevate your skills. You and your squad will build and contribute to live, real-world codebases, gaining the kind of hands-on experience that makes recruiters take notice.

**When is the event?**
The event runs from 20th to 30th August.

**Who can participate?**
Students from the 2025-29 batch. You must participate in a team of 3-4 members.

**What is the mode of the event?**
The event is completely ONLINE.

**What are the main focuses of CloneFest 2.0?**
- End-to-end Open Source Development
- Building Production-Ready Software Architecture
- Getting a massive Portfolio Boost!

**Is there a prize pool?**
Yes! We have a 7K Prize Pool up for grabs.
